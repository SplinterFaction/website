The game

Chess with a clock that never stops

Produce, build, expand, fight — and the map control funds the next cycle. Everything else is decoration. SplinterFaction strips the genre back to that loop and then changes the rules underneath it.

Fed vs. Loz · in-development capture

For the Federation, Damage is a loan. For the Alliance, damage is a bill.

Nobody repairs anything. Federation hulls regenerate and their healstations restore what a fight took, so a grinding engagement costs them time rather than army. Loz shields recharge fast — but once a shield collapses, every point of hull damage underneath is gone for the rest of the match.

Loz must make every engagement count. Fed can afford to be wrong, and then come back. Neither faction wants the fight the other one wants.

Seismic contact vs. what was actually there

There is no radar

Seismic sensors tell you that something is moving, somewhere. Not what. Not how many. A still army pings nothing. A cloaked Federation army pings nothing even while it moves.

Scouting isn't a supplement to your radar picture here. Scouting is the picture. If you aren't sending units out to look, you are playing blind, and you'll find out when something arrives.

Design rules

The things we took out

Some of the clearest decisions in SplinterFaction are subtractive. If you've played other games in the Recoil family, these are the adjustments.

No repair

Constructors build and reclaim. They do not fix. A tank that leaves a fight at 40% stays at 40%. Every engagement has a lasting cost, and trading efficiently is everything.

No build assist

One structure, one builder. Factories cannot be assisted. Your production capacity is your number of factories, not your number of constructors.

No radar

Seismic sensors detect movement and nothing else. A stationary army is a hidden army. Quiet is never safe.

No counter web

Damage is damage. No armour classes, no hidden multipliers. What decides fights is numbers, positioning, range and tech — plus two specialist effects you can actually see: heat and disruption.

Free cloak, free shields

Neither faction's signature system costs energy. Cloak everything. Shield everything. Your power budget is for converters and construction.

Nothing chain-explodes

Pack your power plants however you like. One lucky shot will never cascade through a base you spent ten minutes building.
