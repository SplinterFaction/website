If you have never played a real-time strategy game before, you are not behind. You are exactly who this manual was written for.

The manual is a single, self-contained page you can read online, save, or search. Read Parts I through IV before your first game. Play three games badly. Then come back for the rest. That is the intended order and it works.

The five that cost the most games

  1. Not spending your resources. Metal in the bank does nothing.
  2. Not keeping on top of upgrades. Unspent RP is exactly as wasted as unspent metal.
  3. Ignoring the low supply warnings. The advisor tells you, out loud, before it becomes a problem.
  4. Trying to do too much before your economy can support it. Fewer things, completed, beats more things, all half-built.
  5. Never scouting. There is no radar. If you don't look, you don't know.

Troubleshooting, in one line each. Nobody can join your game: forward port 8254, or use an autohost. Won't launch: drivers, then antivirus, then let Coilbox re-verify. Being attacked by something you can't see: you're fighting Fed — sweep with scouts. Units won't repair: they can't, and that's intentional. Constructors won't speed up your factory: same.
