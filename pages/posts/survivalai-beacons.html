SurvivalAI removes the "what is my opponent doing?" problem while keeping every economic and defensive lesson intact. That makes it a teaching mode as much as a game mode.

Co-op first

A team can share a survival map and hold the line together, which is the single best way to bring someone new in: an experienced player handles the pressure while the newcomer learns the economy without being eliminated for a mistake. The convention is one SurvivalAI per player, but it's entirely optional — three against one is gentle, four against four is serious. And SurvivalAI has a great many AI options: wave pacing, difficulty scaling, beacon behaviour. If it's too punishing while you're learning, turn it down.

Waves have a character

Assault comes head-on and wants a solid front. Raid goes for your economy and wants flank coverage. Siege outranges static defence and wants mobile units to go kill it. Air ignores ground defence entirely and wants anti-air before it arrives. Drop lands behind your lines and wants interior defence and a reaction force. Horde is numbers and wants area damage at a choke. Surge waves are the escalation events, larger and telegraphed — prepare during the lull.

Beacons specialise

Beacons aren't identical. Shield beacons reinforce their waves with protection. Cloaking beacons send every unit out cloaked, which with no radar means genuinely invisible until it's on top of you. Accelerator beacons increase frequency or speed. Forge beacons improve unit quality. That creates a priority list: kill the accelerator and you get breathing room, kill the forge and the waves soften, kill the jammer and you can see again. Garrison turrets defend them, so a beacon assault is a real operation. And when you reduce the AI to its last beacon, it goes into overdrive — build up first, then commit fully.

The mode's real difficulty setting

Downtime spent idle. The early waves are gentle deliberately; every second on defence you don't yet need is compounding economy lost. Build defence to a schedule, not to panic. And go on the offensive: defences don't get repaired, so pure turtling loses eventually. Use the lulls to reclaim, rebuild, expand and push out. The dedicated survival panel and the beacon labels overlay show wave timing, composition, and which beacon is which. Keep both on.
