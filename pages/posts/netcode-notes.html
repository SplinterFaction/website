The choice

Lockstep is cheap and exact, and punishes one bad connection with a stall for everybody. Rollback is forgiving and expensive, and much harder to keep deterministic across forty units with standing orders attached to them.

We have not decided. When we do it will get its own dispatch, and that one will be tagged.
