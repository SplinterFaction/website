Quiet Cartography does not see units. It sees supply.

The mechanic

Every other faction reveals enemy units within line of sight. Cartography does not: it reveals enemy supply links, everywhere on the map, permanently, from the first second. You know exactly where your opponent's economy runs and nothing at all about what is standing on top of it.

This is a genuinely uncomfortable way to play, and that is the point. You are always acting on complete information about the wrong thing.

Why it nearly got cut

For four months it was unplayable. Perfect economic information plus a weak army meant the correct strategy was always to avoid engagement entirely and cut links until the opponent stalled. It won, it was miserable to play against, and it was boring to play.

What fixed it was making supply links repairable. Cutting one now buys you ninety seconds, not a win, so Cartography has to convert that window into position rather than banking it.

Cartography is not the beginner faction. If you are new, start with the Ninth Column — it forgives more.
