The old economy was a number that went up. The new one is a chain of things on the map that an opponent can cut.

What changed

Until build 0.34 you gathered from nodes and the resource counter climbed. It worked, it was legible, and it made every map play identically after the first four minutes. Whoever took more nodes had more army. That is not a strategy game, that is arithmetic with explosions.

Now every structure draws supply through a physical link back to a depot, and every depot links back to your command vehicle. The links are drawn on the map. Both players can see them. Cutting one does not damage anything — it just means the far end stops producing.

What it broke

Almost everything, briefly. Turtling stopped working, which we expected. Rushing also stopped working, which we did not: a rush that outruns its own depot line arrives with nothing behind it and dies to a counterattack from units that were already there.

The interesting part is what it did to retreating. Falling back used to be free. Now falling back past your last depot means abandoning the structures on the far side of it, so the decision has a price you can see before you make it.

Where it is going

Depot Crews are too cheap and we know it. The next build takes them from 60 to 90 and gives them a longer build time, which should stop the current pattern of just spamming redundant links until the network is uncuttable.
